Work top to bottom: an earlier row makes later numbers meaningless. To work through an unstable example or a ported task with evidence at each step, follow the [debug-instability](/workflows/debug-instability/) workflow.

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `check_constraint_capacity()` raises; overflow warning; `*_overflow_world_steps` &gt; 0 | Capacity overflow | Size the family from `*_raw_high_water` ([Capacities](/concepts/capacities/)). Re-measure after any friction or response change: point friction needs more rows than anchors. While a family overflows, the dropped set changes from step to step and can add energy until the state diverges. |
| Contact count equals the buffer size | Contact buffer full | Raise `rigid_contact_max` on the pipeline. |
| Kernels fail to build with very large immediate capacities | More rows than one world's immediate kernel holds | Colored propagation with `propagation_constraint_max`. |
| A start pose or velocity is lost on the first step | Written to `body_q`/`body_qd` only | Write `joint_q`/`joint_qd`. |
| Joints far past their limits | `enable_joint_limits=False`, or a DISTANCE joint, whose limits Steel ignores | Turn limits on. Build a tether from a ball joint and a limited slider ([Tethers](/tutorials/tether/)). |
| A load sinks through a link held by a limit or drive | `row_schedule="contact_then_internal"` | `"interleaved"` or `"phased"`; more iterations do not help ([Row schedule](/tutorials/row-schedule/)). |
| **Jitter** of resting objects | Indeterminate load splits, warm-start overshoot, too few iterations | Keep friction anchors on; try `contact_regularization=0.01`; raise `iterations`; enable sleeping for settled piles. |
| Resting bodies never fall asleep in many worlds | Contact-order jitter at the default `sleep_angular_threshold` | `CollisionPipeline(deterministic=True)`, or `sleep_angular_threshold=0.3` ([Sleeping](/concepts/sleeping/)). |
| A box near its friction limit slides with `"bisection"` or `"bisection_desaxce"` | Radial projection in those modes | `"fixed_normal"`, or `"coulomb_newton"` for free bodies ([Friction modes](/tutorials/friction-mode/)). |
| **Tunneling** or deep overlap of fast, small or thin objects | Travel per step far beyond the contact gap | More substeps, or a larger gap on the thin shape; keep `contact_speculative_scale=1` and no `contact_gap_gate`. |
| **Slow convergence**: stacks sag, chains stretch, grasps slip | Iteration-limited | More iterations, Nesterov momentum, fewer substeps with more iterations, pre-elimination for loops. |
| A chain of light links gains energy or goes NaN at any iteration count | Explicit velocity-product forces, first order in $h$ | A smaller step; armature large against the links' inertia. A longer `update_mass_matrix_interval` lost energy instead on the measured chains ([Step pipeline](/concepts/step-pipeline/), [Armature](/tutorials/armature/)). |
| Passive joint damping diverges within a few steps | Explicit damping past $h\,c\,\lambda_{\max}(\tilde H^{-1}) < 2$ | Lower the damping or the step, or use a drive's `target_kd`, which is implicit ([Joint springs and damping](/tutorials/springs-damping/)). |
| Bodies launched after a hard contact | `baumgarte` too high, or warm start on wedged contacts | `baumgarte` ≤ 0.5; `velocity_iterations`; warm start off. |
| Light bodies pressed into the ground by a heavy one | Mass ratio above about 1000:1 | See the recipe in [Tuning](/guides/tuning/#recipes). |
| Frozen scene with odd substeps under a graph | The captured state swap ends on the other buffer | Use an even substep count when swapping states inside a graph. |
| `NotImplementedError` at construction | Particles, gradients, a CUDA-only feature on CPU, or an unsupported joint layout | See [What does Steel reject at construction?](/faq/#what-does-steel-reject-at-construction). |
| `Loop-closing joints are not supported with articulated_contact_response=...` | Loop closures under propagation | Immediate response, serial sweep. |
| `nesterov_momentum requires solve_mode='serial' ...` | Momentum outside the serial immediate sweep | Serial, immediate, `row_schedule="interleaved"`. |
| Contact torsion construction error | Torsion radii authored without device preparation | `contact_torsion_device=True`, and `velocity_iterations=0` under propagation. |
