Allocate observable arrays once, before capture, and pass them to every step:

```python
from newton.solvers import SolverSteel

solver = SolverSteel(model, enable_sleeping=True)
obs = solver.observables(
    {SolverSteel.ObservableKind.BODY_PARENT_F,
     SolverSteel.ObservableKind.CONTACT_F,
     SolverSteel.ObservableKind.BODY_AWAKE},
    contacts=contacts,
)
solver.step(state_0, state_1, control, contacts, dt, observables=obs)
obs.body_parent_f   # (body_count,) spatial vectors: force [N], torque [N·m], world frame
obs.contact_f       # per rigid contact row: force on shape 0's body [N]
obs.body_awake      # (body_count,) bool
```

| Observable | What it reports | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| `body_parent_f` | The wrench the parent applies to each body through its inbound joint, from the step's solved motion: inverse dynamics at the post-solve accelerations, less gravity, `body_f`, and the solved contact, friction, spin and loop-closure impulses of its subtree divided by $h$. Linear force first, torque about the body's center of mass second. | Built from rigid inertia without armature, like MuJoCo. Loop-closure reactions are left out with pre-elimination. Contact wrenches are summed with atomics, so the last bits can vary run to run. Bodies outside every articulation report zero. |
| `contact_f` | Per live rigid contact row, the force on shape 0's body from the solved normal and friction impulses divided by $h$. | Torque part is zero (torsion is not reported). Dropped rows, rows beyond the contact count and contacts of skipped sleeping islands report zero. |
| `body_awake` | `True` for awake bodies, `False` for sleeping ones, as of the end of the step that received the container. | Requires `enable_sleeping`. Bodies welded to the world always read `True`. |
| `constraint_overflow` | Device bool per world plus a global entry: rows, contacts or input lost since the last `reset`. | Read in a kernel, or call `check_constraint_capacity()` outside capture. |
| `constraint_row_watermarks()` | Per family: retained and requested high-water marks, dropped contact rows, overflow excess, overflowing world-steps; and `contact_high_water`. | Needs `row_watermark=True`; host read outside capture. |
| `compliance_contact_count` | Compliant contacts prepared in the last step, and `compliance_skipped_contact_count` for those excluded. | `contact_compliance` only; host read outside capture. |

Tendon observables do not exist at this commit; Steel does not simulate tendons yet.
